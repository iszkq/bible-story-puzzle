## 共用制作约束

Use case: illustration-story. Complete scene illustrations for an adult iOS Bible jigsaw game. Mature hand-painted storybook art, believable adult proportions, controlled medium detail, natural cloth and environment textures, consistent painterly brushwork. Landscape 4:3. Clear foreground, middle ground, and background. The person named in the scene is visually legible at phone width and important props remain inside a central square-safe area. One coherent natural light source. Rich enough regional texture and contours for 12/24-piece puzzles, without large uniform areas. No text, UI, labels, borders, signatures, watermarks, halos, modern objects, toy proportions, duplicated people, malformed hands, or unrelated biblical events. Keep the illustration colors independent of the blue/gray UI. Generate the complete scene; do not generate separate jigsaw fragments.

## 01 摩西：海中的道路（先做样板）

Primary request: Moses leading people across a dry path between the parted sea waters, based on Exodus 14:15–31. Moses and his staff are clearly visible in the foreground, with coherent anatomy and a natural guiding pose. He occupies about 30–45 percent of the image height. People move through the middle-ground path into the distance. Blue-green sea walls have naturally varied wave shapes and highlights; the sandy dry path has stones and footprints. Warm natural light illuminates people and water consistently. Preserve visual differences across all corners for a jigsaw puzzle. Historically informed ancient clothing, no modern objects or European medieval costumes. Use the common production constraints above.
